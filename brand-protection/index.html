Statutory & Operational FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key legal citations, compliance timelines, and procedural guidelines.

The ™ symbol indicates an unregistered trademark for which an application has been filed or rights are claimed in commerce. The ® symbol can only be legally displayed after the Trade Marks Registry has granted a final Certificate of Registration.
Trademarks are categorized into 45 international classes: Classes 1 to 34 cover physical goods, and Classes 35 to 45 cover services. SaaS, software, and tech platforms typically protect Classes 9, 35, 38, and 42.
Section 9 objections relate to absolute grounds (mark lacks distinctiveness or is purely descriptive of the goods/services). Section 11 objections pertain to relative grounds (identical or deceptively similar to an earlier registered or pending mark).
Once advertised in the Trade Marks Journal, a statutory 4-month public opposition window opens under Section 21 of the Trade Marks Act 1999.
A registered trademark is valid for 10 years from the date of application and can be renewed indefinitely every 10 years via Form TM-R.
