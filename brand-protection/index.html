Frequently Asked Questions

Statutory & Operational Clarifications

The ™ symbol indicates an unregistered trademark application has been filed. The ® symbol can only be legally displayed after the Trade Marks Registry has granted a final Certificate of Registration.
Trademarks are categorized into 45 international classes: Classes 1-34 for physical goods, Classes 35-45 for services.
Section 9 objections relate to absolute grounds (lacking distinctiveness). Section 11 objections pertain to relative grounds (similarity with earlier marks).
A statutory 4-month public opposition window opens once advertised in the Trade Marks Journal.
10 years from the date of application, renewable indefinitely every 10 years via Form TM-R.
