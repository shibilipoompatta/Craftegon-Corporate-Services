Who Needs GST Registration in India?

📦

Turnover Threshold

Businesses with annual turnover exceeding ₹40 Lakhs for Goods and ₹20 Lakhs for Services (₹20L/₹10L in Special Category States).

🌐

Inter-State Supply

Any business or individual supplying goods across state borders requires mandatory GST registration irrespective of total turnover.

🛒

E-Commerce Sellers

Individuals or enterprises selling goods through online marketplaces such as Amazon, Flipkart, or Meesho must register under Section 24.

💼

Casual / Non-Resident

Temporary seasonal vendors, exhibition participants, or non-resident persons making taxable supplies in India.

Checklist

Documents Required for GST Registration

1. Proprietor / Director KYC

  • • PAN Card of Proprietor / Partners / Directors
  • • Aadhaar Card (For OTP Mobile Authentication)
  • • Passport-sized Photograph

2. Business Place Proof

  • • Electricity Bill / Property Tax Receipt
  • • Rent Agreement + Landlord NOC (If rented)
  • • Ownership deed (If owned property)

3. Entity Proof (If applicable)

  • • Partnership Deed (For Partnership firms)
  • • Certificate of Incorporation, MOA & AOA (Pvt Ltd)
  • • Board Resolution / Letter of Authorization
