Mandatory Threshold Limits

Who Needs GST Registration in India?

₹40 Lakhs

Goods Suppliers

Businesses engaged in intraday or intra-state supply of goods exceeding ₹40 Lakhs turnover annually (₹20 Lakhs for Special Category States).

₹20 Lakhs

Service Providers

Service providers, freelancers, CAs, and IT consultants whose gross billing crosses ₹20 Lakhs in a financial year (₹10 Lakhs for Special Category States).

Mandatory

E-Commerce & Interstate Traders

Any business selling goods out of state or through e-commerce marketplaces (Amazon, Flipkart, Shopify) requires GST regardless of turnover.

Documents Required for GST Registration

💳 Applicant Identity & Address Proof

  • • PAN Card of Applicant / Business Firm
  • • Aadhaar Card (Linked with active Mobile Number for E-KYC OTP)
  • • Passport size photo of Proprietor / Partners / Directors
  • • Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed (If not Proprietorship)

🏢 Principal Place of Business Proof

  • • Electricity Bill / Property Tax Receipt of business premise
  • • Rent Agreement / Lease Deed (In case of rented premises)
  • • No Objection Certificate (NOC) signed by the property owner
  • • Cancelled Cheque or Bank Passbook front page
