Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Corporate Questions

Clear, transparent answers to help you navigate Indian legal, tax, and secretarial mandates.

How long does Private Limited Company Incorporation take in India?

Through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) SPICe+ digital portal, incorporation typically completes in 7 to 10 working days. This includes Certificate of Incorporation (COI), PAN, TAN, DIN allotment, and PF/ESIC registrations with no physical office visits required.

What are the mandatory monthly and annual GST filings for businesses?

Regular GST registered taxpayers must file monthly GSTR-1 (outward supplies, due 11th of every month) and GSTR-3B (summary tax liability and ITC claim, due 20th). Annually, businesses must file GSTR-9 along with CA-certified GSTR-9C reconciliation statements for turnovers exceeding statutory limits.

What is India's DPDP Act 2023 compliance and who needs it?

The Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 applies to any enterprise processing digital personal data in India. Compliance mandates transparent bilingual consent notices, establishing Data Protection Officers (DPOs), implementing consent managers, and maintaining audit logs to avoid penalties reaching up to ₹250 Crores.

What is the procedure for registering a trademark under Nice Classes?

Registration begins with an exhaustive phonetic and Vienna code search across Classes 1-45. Once verified, Form TM-A is electronically filed on the IP India portal. The applicant receives an official application number within 24 hours, granting immediate legal rights to use the ™ symbol.

What is the difference between ROC Form AOC-4 and MGT-7?

Form AOC-4 is used to file the company's audited balance sheet, profit and loss account, and director's report with the ROC within 30 days of the AGM. Form MGT-7 / 7A is the company's annual return detailing shareholding structure, directorship changes, and board meetings filed within 60 days of the AGM.

Can a foreign parent company set up a 100% Wholly Owned Subsidiary in India?

Yes. Under the Automatic Route of India's FDI Policy, up to 100% foreign equity is permitted in most sectors without prior government approval. CCS handles apostille/consular document vetting, RBI FIRMS portal registration, Form FC-GPR filing, and resident director onboarding.
