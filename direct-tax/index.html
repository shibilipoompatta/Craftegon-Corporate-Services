Statutory & Operational FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key legal citations, compliance timelines, and procedural guidelines.

Private and Public Limited companies mandatorily file Form ITR-6 electronically with a digital signature under Section 139(1). Limited Liability Partnerships file Form ITR-5.
Under Section 115BAA, domestic corporate entities can opt for a concessional base tax rate of 22% (effective 25.17% including 10% surcharge and 4% cess), subject to forgoing specific exemptions/deductions. Standard rate remains 25% for companies with turnover up to ₹400 Cr in FY 2022-23.
Tax audit is compulsory when total business sales or turnover exceeds ₹1 Crore. The threshold extends to ₹10 Crore if cash receipts and cash payments do not exceed 5% of aggregate transactions.
TDS returns (Form 24Q for salaries, Form 26Q for non-salaries) are due quarterly: Q1 by July 31, Q2 by October 31, Q3 by January 31, and Q4 by May 31.
Form 15CA is an online declaration filed by remitters for outbound international payments. Form 15CB is a statutory certification issued by an independent Chartered Accountant verifying taxability and DTAA rates.
