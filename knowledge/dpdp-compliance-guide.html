Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP 2023): Enterprise Compliance Guide

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) governs the processing of digital personal data within India. It establishes stringent obligations for corporate Data Fiduciaries and introduces penalties up to ₹250 Crores for data breaches.

1. Eligibility Criteria & Statutory Requirements

Prior to initiating electronic filings on government portals (MCA V3, GSTN, or IP India), ensure full compliance with the following statutory parameters:

  • ✓Any business entity collecting, storing, processing, or transmitting digital personal data of Indian citizens.
  • ✓E-commerce platforms, SaaS providers, FinTech, EdTech, healthcare apps, and corporate employers.
⚙️

2. Step-by-Step Filing Procedure

Our streamlined end-to-end execution workflow designed to minimize rejection queries and achieve fastest statutory approval:

1

Personal Data Inventory & Mapping

Catalog all customer, vendor, and employee data flows across digital databases, cloud servers, and CRM tools.

2

Revamped Notice & Multilingual Consent Architecture

Implement clear, affirmative consent mechanisms offering data principals itemized opt-in choices in English and scheduled 22 Indian languages.

3

Data Principal Rights Management System

Establish automated internal workflows to fulfill user requests for data access, correction, erasure, and grievance redressal.

4

Appointment of Data Protection Officer (DPO)

Appoint an India-based Data Protection Officer and establish strict technical data security safeguards including encryption at rest.

📁

3. Mandatory Document Checklist

Keep these documents scanned and self-attested in clear high-resolution PDF/JPEG format:

📄Enterprise Data Flow Inventory Register
📄Customer Privacy Policy & User Terms of Service
📄Data Processing Agreements (DPA) with third-party vendors
📄Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Incident Response and Breach Reporting
