Corporate Income Tax (ITR-6) & Tax Audit (Form 3CD): Deadlines & Slabs

Corporate Income Tax compliance requires Indian companies to compute taxable profits under the Income Tax Act, 1961, claim allowable depreciation and deductions, and file Form ITR-6 alongside mandatory Tax Audit Reports.

1. Eligibility Criteria & Statutory Requirements

Prior to initiating electronic filings on government portals (MCA V3, GSTN, or IP India), ensure full compliance with the following statutory parameters:

  • ✓All domestic and foreign companies registered in India.
  • ✓Tax Audit is mandatory if business turnover exceeds ₹10 Crores (provided digital transactions exceed 95%) or ₹1 Crore (for cash businesses).
⚙️

2. Step-by-Step Filing Procedure

Our streamlined end-to-end execution workflow designed to minimize rejection queries and achieve fastest statutory approval:

1

Advance Tax Computation & Payment

Estimate annual corporate profits and pay advance tax in 4 quarterly installments (June 15, Sept 15, Dec 15, March 15).

2

Statutory Tax Audit (Section 44AB)

Independent Chartered Accountant conducts tax audit and electronically uploads Form 3CA/3CD on the e-filing portal.

3

Tax Audit Approval by Assessee

Director reviews and approves the submitted Form 3CD on the portal using corporate DSC.

4

ITR-6 Computation & Filing

Prepare JSON utility for Form ITR-6, reconcile with AIS/TIS, pay self-assessment tax, and file with DSC.

📁

3. Mandatory Document Checklist

Keep these documents scanned and self-attested in clear high-resolution PDF/JPEG format:

📄Audited Financial Statements (P&L, Balance Sheet, Notes to Accounts)
📄Form 26AS, AIS (Annual Information Statement), and TIS
📄Challans for Advance Tax, Self-Assessment Tax, and TDS payments
📄Depreciation Schedule under Income Tax Rules (Rule 5)
⏳

4. Post-Registration Compliance Slabs

Failure to adhere to post-registration filing dates attracts heavy recurring statutory fines and director disqualification risks:

September 30Tax Audit Report (Form 3CA/3CD) Due Date
October 31Corporate Income Tax Return (ITR-6) Due Date for audited entities
November 30ITR-6 Due Date for companies subject to Transfer Pricing (Form 3CEB)
