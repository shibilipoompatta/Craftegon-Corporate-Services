Trademark Registration in India: Nice Classification, Search & Legal Process

A registered trademark grants 10 years of statutory brand protection under the Trade Marks Act, 1999, providing exclusive proprietary rights to brand names, logos, slogans, and sounds across 45 Nice Classification classes.

1. Eligibility Criteria & Statutory Requirements

Prior to initiating electronic filings on government portals (MCA V3, GSTN, or IP India), ensure full compliance with the following statutory parameters:

  • ✓Individuals, Sole Proprietorships, Startups, LLPs, Private Limited, and Public Limited Companies.
  • ✓Non-governmental organizations, trusts, and foreign entities.
  • ✓Marks that are distinctive, non-descriptive, and not phonetically or visually deceptively similar to earlier marks.
⚙️

2. Step-by-Step Filing Procedure

Our streamlined end-to-end execution workflow designed to minimize rejection queries and achieve fastest statutory approval:

1

Comprehensive Trademark Search

Search IP India public database to verify that the mark is distinctive and has no prior conflicting registrations or pending applications.

2

Class Selection & TM-A Filing

Select proper classes (Goods: Class 1-34, Services: Class 35-45) and electronically file Form TM-A with IP India portal.

3

Examination Report Review

The Trade Marks Registry examines the mark under Section 9 (absolute grounds) and Section 11 (relative grounds) within 1 to 3 months.

4

Publication in Trade Marks Journal

If accepted, the mark is advertised in the official Trade Marks Journal for a statutory 4-month public opposition period.

5

Registration Certificate Issuance

If no opposition is filed or opposition is decided in your favor, the Registrar issues the Digital Registration Certificate (granting ® rights).

📁

3. Mandatory Document Checklist

Keep these documents scanned and self-attested in clear high-resolution PDF/JPEG format:

📄Logo / Brand Artwork in clean high-resolution format
📄Applicant Identity Proof (PAN & Aadhaar / COI)
📄Power of Attorney / Authorization Form TM-48
📄User Affidavit with documentary proof of prior usage (if mark is already in use)
⏳
