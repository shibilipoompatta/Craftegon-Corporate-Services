Statutory & Operational FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key legal citations, compliance timelines, and procedural guidelines.

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, data fiduciaries must provide clear multilingual consent notices, process data only for declared purposes, implement technical safeguards, honor data principal rights, and report breaches within prescribed windows.
The DPDP Act 2023 prescribes monetary penalties up to ₹250 Crore for failure to take reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breaches, and up to ₹200 Crore for failure to notify breaches.
Yes. Under Section 4 of the POSH Act 2013, any workplace employing 10 or more employees must mandatorily constitute an Internal Committee headed by a senior woman employee and an external member.
Under Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act 1872, agreements in restraint of trade are void. Non-compete covenants are enforceable during active employment, but post-employment restrictions are largely unenforceable; non-solicitation and confidentiality clauses provide enforceable alternatives.
EPFO registration is compulsory for establishments employing 20 or more persons. ESIC registration applies to establishments with 10 or more employees earning up to ₹21,000/month in notified areas.
