## Key Points
Reduced withholding tax on dividend income from foreign sources
Lower tax rates on interest earned from overseas investments
Beneficial treatment of royalty and technical service fees
Capital gains tax relief under specific DTAA provisions
Exemption from double taxation on business profits
Credit for foreign taxes paid in India
**Annual Requirement:** A fresh TRC application must be submitted for each assessment year where treaty benefits are required
**Effective Period:** The certificate is effective from April 1st to March 31st of the relevant financial year
**No Automatic Renewal:** There is no facility for automatic renewal; each application must be processed separately
**Income Period Coverage:** The certificate must cover the period during which foreign income was earned or received
**Treaty Claim Timing:** The certificate should be available before claiming treaty benefits in foreign jurisdictions
