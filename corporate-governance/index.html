Frequently Asked Questions

Companies Act 2013 · MCA V3 · ROC Compliance

Private limited companies must file Form AOC-4 (financial statements) and Form MGT-7 or MGT-7A (annual return) every year. MGT-7A applies to small companies and OPCs. These must be filed within 60 days of the AGM. Additionally, DIR-3 KYC is required for all directors annually by 30th September. Under MCA V3, all forms are filed digitally with DSC (Digital Signature Certificate).

DIR-3 KYC is the annual Know Your Customer compliance for directors holding a DIN (Director Identification Number). It must be filed by 30th September every year. Failure to file results in DIN deactivation, preventing the director from signing any MCA V3 documents. Filing requires Aadhaar-linked mobile OTP and email OTP. Late filing attracts a ₹5,000 penalty for reactivation.

Form AOC-4 is used for filing financial statements (Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow, Auditor's Report) with the Registrar of Companies. MGT-7A is the simplified Annual Return form for small companies (paid-up share capital ≤ ₹2 crore and turnover ≤ ₹20 crore) and OPCs. Larger companies file MGT-7 with complete shareholding patterns, indebtedness, and key managerial personnel details.

Missing ROC filing deadlines attracts additional fees of ₹100 per day per form (small companies) or ₹200/day with no upper cap (others). Prolonged non-compliance can lead to: company being marked as defaulting, directors disqualified under Section 164(2) of the Companies Act 2013, and eventual striking off under Section 248. Disqualified directors cannot be appointed in any other company for 5 years.

Within the same city/town: Board Resolution + Form INC-22. To another city in same state: Board Resolution + Form INC-22 + newspaper publication. Inter-state change: Special Resolution + newspaper publication + NOC from creditors + Regional Director (RD) approval + Form INC-23. The process takes 4–8 weeks for inter-state changes and 2–3 weeks for intra-state changes.

A director submits a written resignation to the board. The director files Form DIR-11 (resignation intimation) and the company files Form DIR-12 (change in directors) with ROC within 30 days. The resignation takes effect from the date specified in the letter or the date DIR-11 is filed, whichever is later. Resignation does not relieve the director of liabilities accrued before resignation.

MOA/AOA amendment requires a Special Resolution by shareholders at an EGM or AGM, followed by filing Form MGT-14 (for special resolution) within 30 days. ROC typically processes and updates within 2–4 weeks. For capital clause amendments (increase of authorized capital), Form SH-7 is additionally required. The entire process from EGM notice to ROC confirmation takes approximately 45–60 days.

Form INC-22A (ACTIVE — Active Company Tagging Identities and Verification) was a one-time mandatory filing for all companies incorporated before 31st December 2017. It required tagging the registered office address with geo-coordinates and an office photograph. Companies marked 'ACTIVE non-compliant' faced restrictions on all ROC filings. This was a one-time exercise now closed, but its filing history is recorded in the MCA registry.
