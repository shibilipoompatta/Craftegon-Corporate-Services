Document Checklist

What You Need to Get Started

👤 For Directors / Shareholders

  • • PAN Card (Mandatory for Indian Nationals)
  • • Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License)
  • • Address Proof (Bank Statement or Utility Bill not older than 2 months)
  • • Passport Size Photo

🏢 For Registered Office Address

  • • Electricity Bill / Gas Bill / Water Bill for premises
  • • Rental Agreement (If rented) or Property Deed
  • • No Objection Certificate (NOC) from property owner
