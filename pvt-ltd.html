Verification Checklist

Documents Required for Incorporation

For All Directors / Shareholders

  • • PAN Card (Mandatory for Indian Nationals)
  • • Identity Proof: Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License
  • • Residential Proof: Bank Statement or Utility Bill (< 2 months old)
  • • Passport-sized Photograph

For Registered Business Office

  • • Electricity Bill / Water Bill / Property Tax Receipt
  • • Rent Agreement + Landlord NOC (If premises rented)
  • • Ownership deed or property title (If owned premises)
  • • Complete Registered Office Address Verification
