Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about company formation in India — citing statutory provisions.

SPICe+ (Simplified Proforma for Incorporating Company Electronically Plus) is an integrated web form introduced by MCA under Companies Act, 2013. It enables simultaneous applications for DIN, name reservation, incorporation, PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC, bank account, and GST registration. Part A handles name reservation; Part B handles incorporation. It replaced INC-29 and brings timelines down to 1–3 days for clean applications. CraftEgon CS manages the full SPICe+ lifecycle, ensuring MCA V3 portal compliance.
Under SPICe+ MCA V3, incorporation typically takes 7–10 working days from document submission. Simple incorporations with clean KYC and pre-approved names can be done in 3–5 working days. Delays may arise from DSC issues, DIN complications, or ROC queue backlogs. CraftEgon CS provides real-time milestone tracking throughout. We alert you within 2 hours of Certificate of Incorporation (COI) issuance so you can begin business operations immediately.
There is NO minimum paid-up capital requirement for a Private Limited Company under Companies Act, 2013, as amended in 2015. You may incorporate with ₹1 paid-up. Authorized capital (declared upfront) determines MCA filing fees and stamp duty — starting at ₹1,00,000 keeps stamp duty minimal. We recommend ₹1,00,000 authorized capital with ₹10,000–₹1,00,000 paid-up for most startups. For OPC and LLP, no minimum capital applies either.
Yes. Foreign nationals and NRIs can be directors in an Indian Private Limited Company. However, at least one director must be a Resident Director (stayed ≥182 days in India in the previous calendar year) per Section 149(3) of Companies Act, 2013. Foreign directors need notarized/apostilled identity and address proof, and must obtain a DIN. Where foreign shareholding exceeds 50%, FDI Policy guidelines under FEMA and RBI/DPIIT approvals may apply. CraftEgon CS coordinates the full foreign director onboarding process.
AGILE-PRO-S enables newly incorporated companies to simultaneously apply for GST registration, EPFO/ESIC registration, a bank account, and professional tax registration (in applicable states) alongside the SPICe+ form. This eliminates multiple post-incorporation visits and filings. CraftEgon CS includes AGILE-PRO-S filing in all business setup packages at no additional fee, ensuring your company is GST-registered from Day 1 of incorporation.
Required documents: (1) PAN Card of all designated partners; (2) Aadhaar / Passport / Voter ID for identity proof; (3) Bank statement or utility bill (≤2 months old) for address proof; (4) Passport photographs; (5) DSC (Digital Signature Certificate); (6) Registered office — NOC from owner, utility bill, rent agreement; (7) Subscriber sheet with contribution amounts; (8) LLP Agreement (filed within 30 days of incorporation via Form LLP-3). Foreign nationals require notarized, apostilled documents legalized for India.
Form INC-20A is the Declaration for Commencement of Business under Section 10A of Companies Act, 2013. Every company incorporated on or after 2 November 2018 must file it within 180 days of incorporation, confirming that subscribers have paid the value of shares. Non-filing attracts a penalty of ₹50,000 on the company and ₹1,000/day on every defaulting officer, with the ROC empowered to initiate strike-off. CraftEgon CS includes INC-20A filing in its post-incorporation compliance tracker.
Key compliances post-incorporation: (1) INC-20A within 180 days; (2) Auditor appointment via ADT-1 within 30 days; (3) First Board Meeting within 30 days; (4) Bank account and share capital deposit; (5) GST registration if threshold applies; (6) Annual ROC filings — MGT-7 (Annual Return) and AOC-4 (Financial Statements); (7) DIR-3 KYC annually; (8) Income Tax Return; (9) Statutory Registers maintenance; (10) AGM within 6 months of year-end. Our compliance calendar sends alerts 30 days before every deadline.
