Statutory Provisions

Current Legal Framework & Regulatory Mandate

Limited Liability Partnerships in India are governed under the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 as amended by the LLP (Amendment) Act, 2021, which decriminalized minor procedural defaults.

Incorporation is executed via the FiLLiP (Form for Incorporation of Limited Liability Partnership) web form on the MCA V3 portal:
  • Name Reservation: RUN-LLP service enabling two proposed name choices reflecting permitted business activities.
  • DPIN / DIN Allocation: Up to two Designated Partners can obtain DPIN directly through FiLLiP without prior standalone filing.
  • LLP Agreement (Form 3): Governed by Section 23 of the LLP Act; must be drafted, stamped per state stamp act rules, and filed within 30 days of registration.
  • Permanent Establishment: Direct issuance of LLPIN, PAN, and TAN on incorporation certificate.

Statutory Overview

Governing Act: Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 & LLP (Amendment) Act, 2021 (FiLLiP, Form 3)
Government Portal: Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA V3) / Official Portals
Filing Authority: Registrar of Companies (ROC) / Central Board
Standard Turnaround: 7-10 Working Days
