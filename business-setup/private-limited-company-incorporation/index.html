Statutory Provisions

Current Legal Framework & Regulatory Mandate

Under Section 7 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rule 12 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014, the incorporation of a Private Limited Company is processed electronically through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) V3 portal via the integrated web form SPICe+ (INC-32).

The SPICe+ architecture encompasses:
  • Part A: Reservation of company name through Reserve Unique Name (RUN) web service.
  • Part B: Unified statutory application covering incorporation, allotment of Director Identification Numbers (DIN), mandatory e-MOA (INC-33) and e-AOA (INC-34).
  • AGILE-PRO-S (INC-35): Simultaneous integration for mandatory GSTIN, EPFO, ESIC, Professional Tax Registration (where applicable), Opening of Scheduled Bank Current Account, and Shops & Establishment Code.
  • PAN & TAN: Automated allocation through NSDL/CBDT database integration embedded within Certificate of Incorporation (COI).

Statutory Overview

Governing Act: Companies Act, 2013 (SPICe+ MCA V3, e-MOA/e-AOA, AGILE-PRO-S)
Government Portal: Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA V3) / Official Portals
Filing Authority: Registrar of Companies (ROC) / Central Board
Standard Turnaround: 7-10 Working Days
