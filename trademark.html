NICE Classification

Popular Trademark Classes in India

The IP India registry classifies goods and services under 45 distinct international classes.

Class 9 & 42

Technology & Software

Mobile apps, SaaS platforms, electronics, computer software, and IT consulting services.

Class 25 & 35

Clothing & Retail

Apparel, footwear, fashion accessories, e-commerce stores, and digital marketing agencies.

Class 29, 30 & 43

Food & Hospitality

Packaged foods, spices, coffee/tea, FMCG goods, restaurants, and cloud kitchens.

Class 3 & 5

Cosmetics & Pharma

Skincare products, perfumes, herbal cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, and health supplements.

Checklist

Documents Required for TM Filing

1. Brand Logo / Artwork

  • • High-resolution PNG/JPG format logo
  • • Exact brand name spelling & font
  • • Tagline / Slogan (if protecting combined)

2. Identity Proof of Applicant

  • • PAN Card & Aadhaar (Individual / Proprietor)
  • • Certificate of Incorporation (Company / LLP)
  • • Partnership Deed (If Partnership Firm)

3. User Affidavit & Fee Discount

  • • MSME / Udyam Certificate (For 50% Govt Discount)
  • • User Affidavit if prior commercial use claimed
  • • Form TM-48 (Power of Attorney authorization)
