Understanding Trademark Classes (NICE 1–45)

Class 9

Software & Tech

Mobile apps, SaaS products, hardware, electronic devices, and AI systems.

Class 25

Clothing & Fashion

Apparel, footwear, streetwear brands, headwear, and fashion accessories.

Class 35

Business & Retail

E-commerce stores, advertising agencies, retail shops, and corporate services.

Class 43

Food & Hospitality

Restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens, hotels, and food delivery services.

Documents Required for Trademark Filing

📄 Basic Registration Details

  • • Name, Address & Nationality of Applicant
  • • PAN Card & Aadhaar Card of Applicant / Authorized Signatory
  • • Certificate of Incorporation / MSME Certificate (for 50% Govt fee discount)
  • • Power of Attorney / Authorization Form 48 (Drafted by Craftegon)

🎨 Brand Assets & Usage Proof

  • • Logo Image (High resolution PNG/JPEG format if registering logo)
  • • User Affidavit (If brand name has been in prior use in India)
  • • Invoice, Website, or Advertisement Proof (For prior use claims)
