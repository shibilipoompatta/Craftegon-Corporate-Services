Statutory & Operational FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key legal citations, compliance timelines, and procedural guidelines.

Under Section 22 of the CGST Act 2017, registration is compulsory when annual turnover exceeds ₹40 Lakhs for goods (₹20 Lakhs in Special Category States) or ₹20 Lakhs for service providers. Compulsory registration without threshold applies to inter-state suppliers, e-commerce vendors, and reverse-charge payers under Section 24.
GSTR-1 (filed by 11th of every month) is the detailed statutory statement of outward supplies and invoice-level details. GSTR-3B (filed by 20th) is the monthly consolidated summary return where tax liabilities are self-assessed, ITC is credited, and net tax is settled via cash or credit ledger.
Under Rule 48(4) of the CGST Rules, electronic invoicing via the Invoice Registration Portal (IRP) is compulsory for all B2B and export transactions of taxpayers whose aggregate turnover exceeds ₹5 Crore in any preceding financial year since 2017-18.
Input Tax Credit cannot be claimed in GSTR-3B unless invoices are uploaded by vendors and accurately reflected in auto-drafted Form GSTR-2B. Non-matching vendor invoices result in statutory disallowance with interest penalties under Section 50.
Filing of annual Form GSTR-9 is compulsory for registered taxpayers with aggregate turnover exceeding ₹2 Crore in the relevant financial year, due by December 31 following the close of the financial year.
