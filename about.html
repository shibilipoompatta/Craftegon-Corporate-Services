Institutional Regulatory Positioning

CraftEgon Corporate Services is proudly positioned as a corporate technology, compliance, and management platform. CCS is not a law firm and does not provide court litigation or judicial representation before courts or tribunals.

All judicial proceedings and tribunal appearances are executed exclusively through independent, licensed advocates or associate law firms in accordance with the Advocates Act, 1961.
